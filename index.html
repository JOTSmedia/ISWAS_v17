it started with a SCREAMSCREAM

A conversation with actors, directors, writers, and special effect makeup artists who found their footing in Hollywood with a horror credit. We explore their stories through sit-down interviews and archival footage of their work. This is not a hosted tour of the genre but an intimate reflection of each subject’s life and career, through a horror focused lens.
