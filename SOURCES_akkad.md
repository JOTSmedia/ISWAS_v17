# ISWAS_v8 — Moustapha Akkad shrine: bio sources

Every fact in the shrine bio was checked against the sources below (fetched 2026-10-06).

| Fact in bio | Sources |
|---|---|
| Born in Aleppo, Syria, July 1930 | Wikipedia (1 July 1930); The Telegraph obituary ("Born at Aleppo, Syria, in July 1930"); Deutsche Biographie (1930-07-01); Library of Congress name authority citing the NYT obituary ("b. July 1930, Aleppo, Syria") |
| Came to the US in 1954 with a few hundred dollars; studied film at UCLA | CNN; Los Angeles Times; The Telegraph ($200, UCLA) |
| Master's degree at USC | Wikipedia; CNN |
| Produced and directed The Message (1976), starring Anthony Quinn | Wikipedia; BBC News; CNN |
| Founded Trancas International Films in 1977 | CNN ("He founded Trancas in 1977"); Wikipedia ("his production company Trancas International Films") |
| With Irwin Yablans, backed John Carpenter and put up the money for Halloween (1978), a low-budget film | Wikipedia, "Halloween (1978 film)"; HalloweenMovies.com (official site); BBC News (2013, Carpenter interview) |
| Halloween introduced Michael Myers and a then-unknown Jamie Lee Curtis | CNN (Curtis's first big-screen film); The Seattle Times (first film featuring Michael Myers) |
| Executive produced all eight Halloween films, through Halloween: Resurrection (2002) | Wikipedia (executive producer, first eight films); BBC News ("executive producer of the Halloween films", "produced all eight", most recent Resurrection, 2002) |
| Directed Lion of the Desert (1981), Quinn as Libyan anti-colonial fighter Omar Mukhtar | BBC News; AP / NBC News; Al Jazeera |
| 9 Nov 2005: with daughter Rima Akkad Monla, 34, in the Grand Hyatt Amman lobby when a suicide bomber struck; Rima killed instantly | AP / NBC News; Wikipedia; The Guardian |
| Died of his injuries on 11 November 2005, aged 75 | Wikipedia; AP / Washington Post ("died Nov. 11"); BBC News (dated 11 Nov 2005: "died in hospital on Friday"); The Guardian (aged 75) |

Note on the birth year: BBC News gives 1935 and the LA Times gives his age as 72, but Wikipedia, the Telegraph, the NYT (via the Library of Congress), the Deutsche Biographie and the AP (aged 75) all agree on July 1930. The bio uses July 1930.
Britannica: no Moustapha Akkad entry exists (britannica.com/biography/Moustapha-Akkad returns 404). Its "Halloween (1978)" topic page is a stub and doesn't name him, so no Britannica fact is used.
